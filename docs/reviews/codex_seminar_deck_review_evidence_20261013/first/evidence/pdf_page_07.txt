MPM
 How the electrolyte parameters were fixed
• Bulk modulus from DFT; shear softening calibrated to pure-electrolyte compaction; 
yield stress from the LPSCl literature range.
Three layers of the SE model
• Real bulk: E ≈ 24 GPa, σy 0.05–0.30 GPa 
(single-crystal LPSCl range)
• DEM effective: E = 1.35 GPa (18× 
softened) — reproduces compaction, not a 
property
• MPM: E = 1.53 GPa, ν = 0.49, σy = 0.30 
GPa; K = 25.5 GPa ≈ DFT bulk modulus 
26.2 GPa
What is — and is not — an external 
anchor
• Pure-SE porosity 10 % at 300 MPa is the 
calibration target: a lab reference sample, 
unpublished
• It is not a literature anchor; the earlier 
citation was retracted
• Shear-only softening keeps the real bulk 
modulus (ν = 0.49); softening the bulk over-
crushed the bed
The softening is irreducible for a resolved-grain continuum — a volumetric cap and real E 
both failed the Heckel test.
