 What the pipeline can and cannot say today
• DEM + contact network: composition trends in porosity, Li⁺ transport and 
load sharing — one bed per composition, model-internal ratios.
• MPM: plastic morphology and the stress-stop thickness; porosity agreement 
is not independent evidence.
• Voxel transport: a contact-free upper branch; electronic gains are grid-
dependent and absolute values exceed measurements.
• Every production number is pre-registered, sealed and externally re-verified 
before it is reported.
