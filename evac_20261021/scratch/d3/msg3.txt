Mixer D3 design v2: fix the broken guarantees (MXD3-01..21), lab numbers

New canonical design docs/reviews/mixer_d3_se_flowability_anchor_design_v2_20261021.md.
It keeps the v1 frame and the ratified Q1-Q12, and handles every ledger item
MXD3-01..21 in one table: fix the rule, narrow the guarantee, or leave it to an
author data wait.  Rules R01-R17 carry the ledger numbers:

- R01 intersection check B gets a registered detection limit; above the
  tolerance the anchor is labelled "friction not checked".
- R02 each interpolation cell adds its monotone error bound |dI| to the band,
  with pre-seal refinement of steep cells; R03 anchors only on a single
  connected component (F9 ambiguous, F10 one test empty).
- R04 pooled seed SD with Student t, auxiliary status without two-seed
  levels, same NP, steady state from within-run blocks; R14 day means with
  t(n_days - 1).
- R05 zero wall adhesion at the centre and both ends as the systematic, on
  every test with walls; R06 T2 definability (fit residual, wall wrapping).
- R07 the interval goes to the confirmation-block registration, where Bo* or
  Bo*/G is chosen after the experimental mixer is known.
- R08 rules and SOP are committed before any measurement, raw data are pushed
  encrypted right after measuring, keys after the curve seal.
- R09 validation counts only when it discriminates; R10 runs the contract
  checker cannot read are excluded; R11 test decks keep the three phase types
  with zero AM and registered geometry; R13 one extension to 0.95 x the
  generation ceiling, "friction floor" on a flat bottom; R15 optional powders
  give an upper bound only; R16 wording; R17 pre-result definitions.

Every number is from the lab20261014 generation; gen1 values appear only in the
history table (this closes the D3 part of MXD2-07).  New choices are marked as
awaiting author ratification (Qv2-1..16).
