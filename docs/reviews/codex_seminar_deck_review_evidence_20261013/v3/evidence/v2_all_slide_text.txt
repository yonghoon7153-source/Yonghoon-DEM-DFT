## Slide 5
What a rigid-sphere DEM cannot do
No shape change and no yield cap: overlap is the only proxy for plasticity, so the 18× softened SE modulus (24 → 1.35 GPa) is a surrogate, not a property.
During compression, global viscous damping carries 58 % of the plate reaction in one 7:3 bed — an open issue.

## Slide 7
MPM
How the electrolyte parameters were fixed
Bulk modulus from DFT; shear softening calibrated to pure-electrolyte compaction; yield stress from the LPSCl literature range.
Three layers of the SE model
Real bulk: E ≈ 24 GPa, σy 0.05–0.30 GPa (single-crystal LPSCl range)
DEM effective: E = 1.35 GPa (18× softened) — reproduces compaction, not a property
MPM: E = 1.53 GPa, ν = 0.49, σy = 0.30 GPa; K = 25.5 GPa ≈ DFT bulk modulus 26.2 GPa
What is — and is not — an external anchor
Pure-SE porosity 10 % at 300 MPa is the calibration target: a lab reference sample, unpublished
It is not a literature anchor; the earlier citation was retracted
Shear-only softening keeps the real bulk modulus (ν = 0.49); softening the bulk over-crushed the bed
The softening is irreducible for a resolved-grain continuum
 — a volumetric cap and real E both failed the Heckel test.
NOTES
Sources: CLAUDE.md "3D MPM compaction — 3-fix calibration" (K = E/(3(1−2ν)) = 25.5 GPa vs DFT B0 26.23); claims ledger CL-94 (pure-SE 10 % is a calibration target; the Minnmann citation was retracted); 2026-10-08 note: the 10 % value is a lab standard sample, unpublished. "Heckel test" = DPC/jamming cross-check (docs/mpm_dpc_cap_crosscheck.md).

## Slide 8

## Slide 17
Summary
What the pipeline can and cannot say today
DEM + contact network: 
composition trends in porosity, Li⁺ transport and load sharing — one bed per composition, model-internal ratios.
MPM: 
plastic morphology and the stress-stop thickness; porosity agreement is not independent evidence.
Voxel transport: 
a contact-free upper branch; electronic gains are grid-dependent and absolute values exceed measurements.
Every production number is pre-registered, sealed and externally re-verified
 before it is reported.
