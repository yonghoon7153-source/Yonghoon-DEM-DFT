## §2.2 나노 계면 · 마이크론 두께의 동역학 설계 (p. 4 · 그림 2g,h · 3 · S1–S3 · 표 S1)

- 회로 배정 `[인쇄]`: R1 "resistance of the connected electrolyte network including separator layers, catholyte, and anolyte" · CPE1 "Li diffusion in NMC" · "Two depressed semicircles are fitted at ≈500 Hz (R3-CPE3) and 10 kHz (R2-CPE2)" · 10 kHz = "Li ion transport through solid electrolyte within the electrode composite layers" · 500 Hz = "interface between electrode active material and solid electrolyte in the composite matrices[20–22] at nano to submicron scale".
- 배정 논리(구성 차) `[인쇄]`: "When only using large catholyte particles, we find small R2 (10 kHz) and large R3 (500 Hz). Conversely, when only using small catholyte particles, it shows the opposite trend … We thus conclude that R2 is decided by the Li ion conduction in the catholyte network; while, R3 is decided by the cathode–electrolyte interface contact."
- **Ea 논거** `[인쇄]`: "the cell configuration of mixed catholyte cathode paired with Si–Cl anode gives an Ea of 284 meV, which is 37 meV higher than the large catholyte-only configuration and 86 meV higher than the small catholyte-only configuration. This is because Ea here corresponds to resistivity ρ rather than to resistance R, where σ = 1/ρ ∝ exp(−Ea/kT). The much smaller interface resistance R3 but higher resistivity (ρ) for the mixed catholyte cell than the large catholyte-only cell thus suggests that although adding small LPSCl1.5 particles increases the resistivity at various interfaces, the overall interface contact area (A) is dramatically increased; thus, giving a much lower resistance (R = ρl/A) in the cathode layer, where l is the interface thickness." · mixed ↔ small: "the interface R3 is comparable because both cells now contain the small LPSCl1.5 particles … the existence of large catholyte particles with high bulk conductivity effectively transports Li ions across the thick catholyte network, which dramatically reduces R2." · Si–Cl ↔ Si–G: "the latter is 42 meV larger than the former … adding electrolyte to the anode layer may introduce extra interface reaction to increase the Ea and interface resistivity, but the benefit of the LPSCl1.0 region with much higher bulk ionic conductivity than Si, Li–Si alloy, and graphite effectively reduces the R3 in the latter to 39 Ω (Figure 2g4) from the 51 Ω of the former case (Figure 2g5)." ⇒ **R3 는 양극 계면만이 아니라 음극 쪽 계면도 담는다**(저자 자신이 음극 교체로 R3 가 움직인다고 적음 — `[해석]` "cathode–electrolyte interface contact" 이름표와 긴장).
- 온도(2h) · 사이클(3): `[인쇄]` "Three batteries with different cathode loading from 18 to 27 mg cm⁻² could stably cycle with 2.5 to 4.6 mAh cm⁻² at charge rate ranging from 5 to 3 C for more than 2400 to 4200 cycles" · "oscillating temperature range at RT from 22° to 30 °C without temperature control" · "coulombic efficiency slightly fluctuating around 100%" · "At even higher loading, for example, ≈7 and ≈10 mAh cm⁻², the battery could still cycle at 1 and 0.5 C".
- 탄소 `[인쇄]`: "We do not add carbon additives because at the NMC:catholyte weight ratio of 7:3, electronic conductivity of the percolated NMC network is high enough.[11]" · "at low NMC:LPSCl1.5 weight ratio of 2:8 or NMC:LGPS weight ratio of 4:6, the NMC network loses electronic percolation" · 7:3 에서 CNF 는 mixed · large 를 조금 깎고 small 에만 "marginal benefit … with the highest tortuosity among the three" · "CNF and/or CNF-induced decomposition will only increase the ionic tortuosity."

# 곱 축퇴 처방 — 예순여덟 번째 적용 ([[assb-lampe-contact-product-degeneracy]])

- **입력 점검**: 1단계 입력 있음 — R3 · C3(표 S1 · S2 · Hsu–Mansfeld 재현) × 네 구성 × 네 온도. **신품 · 구성 축**(사이클 축 아님) · 면적 미인쇄(Ω · F) · SOC 미인쇄(G5) · 셀 하나씩 · Q · n 은 표 S2(CPE-T · P).
- **1단계 판독**(전제 `C ∝ 면적` · 같은 ε): τ3 = R3·C3 = ρε 는 면적 · 두께에 무관 → 순수 면적형이면 τ 불변. `[재현]` mixed/large **×2.2**(25 °C) · ×2.5(10) · ×2.6(−5) · ×1.5(55) · small/large ×2.4 · 3.4 · 3.6 · 1.4 ⇒ **화학형 몫이 있다**(ρε ↑) — 저자 "adding small LPSCl1.5 particles increases the resistivity at various interfaces" 와 같은 방향. C3 비(×4.1 · ×5.7)를 면적 비로 읽으면 R3 ×0.54 · ×0.43 은 그 곱(면적 ×4.1 · ρ ×2.2 → R ×0.54)으로 닫힌다 — **동어반복**(R · C 둘로 A · ρ 둘을 정한 것 · 독립 관측 0).
- **3단계-b**(C3/면적 → 이중층 대역): 면적 미인쇄 → 불가. C3 4.8e-6–2.7e-5 F 절대값은 셀 면적 0.3–1.3 cm² 어느 가정에서도 4–90 µF cm⁻²(기하 면적 기준) — NMC 표면적 기준 환산은 입도 분포 0 이라 안 한다.
- **4단계**(DC ↔ EIS): 면적 없이는 닫히지 않음(§(b)③).
- **처방 표 후보 줄**: "온도 기울기(Ea)로 ρ ↔ A 를 갈랐다는 편은 R·C 곱(τ) 비로 교차한다 — τ 가 움직이면 면적만이 아니다; Ea 는 log 밑 · 점 수를 재적합한다".
- ⚠ **이것이 곱을 푼 것은 아니다** — τ 비는 `C ∝ A` · 같은 ε · 신품 · 방전 뒤 SOC 위의 판독이고, R3 자체가 양극 계면만이 아니다(저자: 음극 교체로 51 → 39 Ω).
