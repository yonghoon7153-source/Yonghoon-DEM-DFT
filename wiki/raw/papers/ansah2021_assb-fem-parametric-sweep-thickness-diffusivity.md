## 3 Results and Discussion (p. 537–540)

- **3.1 Model Validation**(그림 2): "a constant current of 0.1 C is employed … The discharge profile begins at a voltage of about 4 V and decreases gradually **owing to the lithium-ion migration in the SE**" — `[재현]` 식 (10) 자체가 θ 0.665 → 0.975 에서 4.02 → 3.80 V 로 기운다(평탄의 기울기 대부분은 OCP 모양 · `[해석]`) · "At a maximum activity value of the lithium concentration … there is a rapid reduction in the discharging voltage" · "experimental data obtained from an experiment performed under a controlled temperature of 298 K" · "The comparison at a discharging rate of 0.1 C is done for 1,5,10 and 20 cycles and there is a high correlation … reflected in the high average R-squared (Pearson correlation) value, 0.96, indicating a good agreement … These results can substantiate the ability of the model to predict the performance of the cell as shown in the subsequent studies."
- **3.2 Electrode Thickness Effect**(그림 3–5): "increasing the thickness of the cathode has been shown to improve the energy density of the cell by minimizing the relative volume of inactive components [35, 36]" · "The cathode thickness used for the experimental studies is 200 μm (Table 1). This cathode thickness is varied up to 600 μm [35, 37]" · 용량 "48.42, 70.80, 92.00, 111.92, 130.66 and 217.71 mAh/g" · "the direct relationship" · "the linear relationship"(D6) · 기구 "an increase in the thickness of the cathode implies more sites created in the structure of the LiCoO2 to be filled by the lithium-ion during the lithiation process [36]" · "for thinner electrodes, the active material of the cathode can be almost fully lithiated easily [35]. Therefore, limiting electrochemical reactions and the transport … Thus, shortening the capacity" — `[해석]` mAh g⁻¹ 가 두께에 비례하는 것은 분모가 고정 질량일 때만 가능하고(G6), 그 경우 '얇은 전극의 작은 용량' 은 물질이 적어서다(이용률이 아니라) · 율 "Since SSB are usually discharged at C-rate between 0.1 and 1 C [16, 18, 39]" · 값(D1) · "due to increased internal resistance in thicker electrodes for high C-rates [14]".
- **3.3 SE Thickness and C_max**(그림 6 · 7): "When the thickness of the SE is reduced from the initial thickness of 700 to 200 μm, there is an overall 41.30% increase in the capacity. However, an increase in the SE thickness to 900 μm leads to a 22.67% decrease"(D4) · "This phenomenon is mainly attributed to the dependency of SE size on the internal resistance of the cell [19]. … The conductance is inversely proportional to the thickness of the SE [8, 19]" · 식 (17) "t = l²/D … Thinner SE … means less time for transportation of lithium-ions across it" · C_max: "when it comes to battery management system (BMS) … the role of the concentration in the solid phase cannot be left out. For this reason, **we decided for the first time** with our model to study the effect of the maximum concentration in the solid phase. Additionally, since the maximum concentration is coupled to the initial concentration of the cell during the model construction …"(G11) · "as the concentration increases to 2380, 2430 and 2530 mol/m3 the SSB capacity also increases to 94.20, 96.50 and 100.80 respectively"(단위 없음) · "high concentration content transports a reasonable amount of lithium to the solid phase (cathode). This means internal resistance within the cell is reduced [41, 42]" · "These results can be a guide to design BMS".
- **3.4 The Role of Diffusivity**(그림 8): "The electrode thickness used and the diffusivity used are both shown in Table 1. The initial diffusivity used is indicated with a dashed line"(D2) · "varied within the range of 1E-18 to 1E-11 m2/s" · "for a discharging rate of 0.1 C, the performance increases as the diffusivity increases up to 1E-15 m2/s. From diffusivity beyond 1E-15 m2/s, the capacity does not change therefore a saturation in performance. However, at 1 C, the cell capacity increases and does not saturate"(D3) · "This result makes the diffusivity a performance-limiting factor of the SSB [7]. The performance limiting factor is simply defined as the factor(s) that constrains the performance of the cell."

## 4 Conclusion (p. 540)

`[인쇄]` "through modeling approach, the influence of the electrode and electrolyte thickness and diffusivity on the performance of SSB is investigated. The model developed was validated with experimental data using COMSOL Multiphysics software (version 5.4). From our validated model, an increase in electrode thickness to 600 μm led to an improvement in the performance of the SSB (The capacity of the cell increases to about 217.71 mAh/g). This was attributed to the enhancement in lithium-ion diffusion path during lithiation." · "a decrease in the thickness of the SE to 200 μm improved the overall performance of the cell to about 41.30% because of the enhanced transport of lithium-ion across thin SE" · "an increase in the maximum concentration of lithium in the solid phase leads to an increase in the capacity of the SSB establishing a direct proportionality between the two parameters"(D5) · "the cathode diffusivity is a performance-limiting factor of the SSB" · "it can also serve as a tool for designing future SSBs based on their applications." — 결론의 조건(0.1 C · 고정 질량 정규화 · 그림 8 의 한 두께 · 인쇄 0 의 구조)이 문장에서 빠진다(§3-b (냐) · (겨)).

# ★ (e) Q5 — 음극 InLi 와 '셀 전압' 의 기준

- 실험 셀(InLi)의 전압은 InLi 기준이면 Li 기준보다 ≈0.62 V(42호 평탄 · 카드 Q5 관례 0.62 / 0.600 — §3-b (오)) 낮다. 그림 2 실험 점은 ≈4.05 V 에서 시작해 2.5 V 로 끝난다 — `[해석]` InLi 기준 4.05 V 는 Li 기준 4.67 V(LiCoO₂ 과충전 영역)라 **Li 기준으로 환산해 그린 값으로 읽히고**(2.5 V 차단 = InLi 1.88 V), 환산 값(0.62 · 0.600) · 방법은 지면 0(G4).

- ⇒ 칸 이동 없음(Q5): 전위 측정 0 · 기준극 0 · 상대극 평탄 · 조성 0. 개념 [[assb-li-in-reference-potential-window]] 의 **서른여섯 번째 형태 — 모형이 In/InLi 상대극을 '무한 원천 · 전위 0' 으로 두고 Li 기준 OCP 로 셀 전압을 계산한 뒤 InLi 셀 실험과 비교하면서 환산을 적지 않고, 그 비교의 지표는 상수 이동에 불변**.

# 이 편이 우리 프로젝트에 주는 것 (정리)

- **'다사이클 곡선이 맞는다' 는 숨은 모드 배정일 수 있다** — 열화 항 없는 모형이 1 · 5 · 10 · 20 사이클 곡선을 사이클별 상태(바꾼 것 인쇄 0)로 따라갔다. 무엇을 바꿨느냐(용량 스케일 ↔ 저항형)가 곧 LAM 형 ↔ 저항 배정이고, 그 선택이 지면에서 지워졌다 — 우리 `degradation-degeneracy/` 물음("곡선이 맞는다 ≠ 분해가 맞다")의 야생 표본(우리 수치는 `degradation-degeneracy/docs/RESULTS*.md` 정본 — 옮기지 않음).
- **상관 지표는 기준 · 정규화 축퇴를 못 본다** — Pearson R² 는 상수 이동 · 척도에 불변이라 InLi ↔ Li 0.62 V · 질량 정규화 오차가 있어도 0.96 이다 → 하네스 · 비교에서 적합 품질은 전압 단위 잔차(RMSE · 최대)와 기준 표기를 같이 적어야 한다(`[해석]`).
- **박막 적합값이 '문헌값' 이 되어 다른 셀로 옮아간다** — 91호(320 nm Li₃PO₄ 박막)의 D_Li · δ · δa₀ · a_max 가 99호를 거쳐 200 µm argyrodite 셀의 표 1 이 되었고, 출처 표기는 다른 번호다 → truth · 하네스 입력 값은 계보(원 셀 · 방법 · 단위 관례)를 같이 적어야 한다(§3-b (여) · (뎌) · (료)).
- **쓸기 결론은 인쇄 기하의 무차원 수로 먼저 거른다** — 확산 시간 263 일 ↔ 방전 10 h 같은 산술 하나가 '두께 ∝ 용량' · '확산 한계' 의 근거를 갈아엎는다(새 판단 거리 3).
- **Q5** — InLi 셀을 Li 기준 모형으로 비교하며 환산을 안 적은 모형 편 표본(개념 [[assb-li-in-reference-potential-window]] 서른여섯 번째 형태).
- **이 편이 주지 않는 것**: 실험 셀 정보(조성 · 적재 · 압력 · 프로토콜) · 접촉 · 박리 · 압력 · LAM · LLI 분해 · 매개변수 추정 방법 · 식별성 · 반복 · 원자료 · 코드 — 그리고 104 편 누적 `θ(N)` 0.
