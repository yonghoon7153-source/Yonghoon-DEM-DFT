## G1 [P1] The new “same amplitude is attainable inside the gamma box” claim fails its own arithmetic and the executable diagnostic

Locations: `FINDINGS.md:1217-1219`; `scripts/ne_shape.py:228-232`; current artifacts `out/ne_shape_GITT_Li.csv:4` and `out/matrix_300_0009_v2.csv`.

The revision divides the measured maximum change by a fitted secant sensitivity and concludes an excursion of 0.07–0.37 is within the box because the **box width** is 0.5. The starting gamma is not an endpoint. Using the current full-precision matrix pair and the committed shape amplitudes:

```text

allowed gamma = [0, 0.5]
allowed delta = [-0.29529879364528394, +0.20470120635471606]

document's required |delta| = 0.36531697272662444
```

Both signs are outside the permitted interval. This directly refutes the document's bound argument. It does **not** establish actual inability of the private-data Blend family: the secant itself is not a global derivative bound, and the maximum-over-x location can change when gamma changes.

The executable script makes the stronger sentence unconditionally whenever `(b)/(a)<0.34`, without checking any bound or evaluating the family's attainable amplitude. A synthetic ordered Si/Gr Blend with `gamma_ref=0.25`, fitted target `0.26`, and measured curve `Blend(x,0.25)+0.1 V` produces:

```text

```

For this synthetic input, the component capacity curves have a fixed ordering, so their convex mixture is monotone in gamma and the family envelope is attained at gamma 0 and 0.5. The script computes that endpoint envelope and independently checks 1,001 gamma values. The quoted maximum is over ne_shape's x=0.02–0.98, 400-point diagnostic grid, not a separately proven continuous-x maximum. This is not a missed optimizer solution.

Minimum correction: remove the unconditional attainable-amplitude claim. Report the secant as a local descriptive ratio only. To assert feasibility, exhibit a legal gamma with the claimed amplitude from the same reference; for shape agreement also evaluate a common alignment and residual, since equal maximum amplitudes are not equal curves. ## Consequences for the new-model design request

The proposed three directions are reasonable **candidates**, but not consequences already forced by these data:

1. Measured-NE representation needs an explicit capacity/stoichiometric-coordinate registration and a statement of what measured half-cell protocol is transferable to each full-cell state. Preserve absolute capacity and quantify half-cell uncertainty; substitution of independently normalized curves is not by itself a validated degradation model.
2. PE handling should be a named experimental choice, with matched fixed/state-specific input comparisons and common reference handling. Report both voltage and derivative consequences on the domain the actual objective consumes.
3. A noise-aware objective needs variance/correlation and model-discrepancy terms distinguished. Derivative residuals are computed from the same voltage data and are correlated with them; giving them an independent loss does not create independent information. The known chain-rule inconsistency must be removed in the new model and validated with synthetic derivative checks.

Before a model is selected, require synthetic known-truth recovery (including zero degradation), equal-family/nonfamily diagnostic controls, and held-out or repeat-measurement discrimination. Document unidentified directions and abstentions. The numerical-method subreview independently checks the R3 claim that rising RMSE excludes noise; do not count that finding twice.
