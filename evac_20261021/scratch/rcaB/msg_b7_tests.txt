fp_sid / fp_sig, but the contract only checked their hex32 shape, and the
sigma binding (certificate sigma = result sigma) binds nothing when both
are 0.  A real bottom-limit (R_j = inf) zero certificate with only its
fingerprints replaced was accepted by conv_cert_problem and
next_gen_cert_ok.  The positive certificate has no fingerprint at all, so
a certificate from another bed or another plan with the same sigma
(within 1e-9) is accepted too.
