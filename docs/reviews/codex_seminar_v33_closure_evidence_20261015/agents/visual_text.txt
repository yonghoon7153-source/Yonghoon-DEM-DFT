DEM → MPM
Luding 2008, Fig. 1 [1] (left) · figure: presenter — MPM 3D view of the DEM-scaffold bed (right)
 Fixed-shape DEM limits and the material point method (MPM)
• Our fixed-shape implementation has no shape change and no yield cap: overlap is the only
plasticity proxy; the softened SE modulus (1.35 vs ≈ 22 GPa) is an effective parameter, not a
property [1, 2].
• J2 plasticity on a background grid: shape change and void filling that a fixed-shape sphere
cannot represent; active material is kept rigid (DEM skeleton), only the electrolyte flows.

This work — voxel transport solver (a, b) · contact network, five NCM–SE beds (c)
 Problems 1 and 2: grid dependence and absolute σ(e⁻)
• The electronic ratio decreases across the three tested voxel spacings (0.4, 0.3, 0.25 µm); the
ionic ratio crosses 1. Grid convergence has not been established.
• Voxel predictions exceed the available DC-polarization estimates; measurement conditions
remain unverified. A registered re-run calibrates carbon on SBE alone (σ_VGCF* = 3.41 S cm⁻¹,
an effective value); the DBE prediction (order only) is pending.

Limits
This work — DEM, one 7:3 bed: stop, relaxation and servo-hold branch
 Problem 4: what the 300 MPa plate reaction contains
• The 300 MPa stop criterion reads the plate reaction during compression, which includes global
viscous damping: in one 7:3 bed the drag is 57.9 % of the target force (750 N, deck units).
• At fixed platen, the original run relaxes to 165 MPa. A combined follow-up protocol—plate-
contact history reset, lower damping and force hold—reduces thickness from 111.05 to
105.84 µm. This remains a model-internal diagnostic; static-equilibrium and conservation
checks are pending.

This work: exploratory contact statistics (left), conditional adhesion model (right)
 Surface energy and coating: SE–SE adhesion is the open term
• With equal contact areas and an assumed geometric-mean adhesion rule, ΔW = 2(√γ_A −
√γ_S)² ≥ 0 is an adhesive-work difference per unit area; SE–SE cluster survival also depends on
relative surface energies and contact dynamics.
• Exploratory all-pair CED scaling retains a larger SE–SE cluster fraction than the low-cohesion
comparator; the confirmatory 18-run block remains on hold.
Left: one seed, mean over the second revolution; whiskers = dump-resolution range, not a confidence interval.
Right: γ = surface energy, W = work of adhesion; the uncoated NCM / SE surface-energy ratio is not known.

Summary
 Current findings and limitations
• DEM and contact networks: composition trends from one bed per composition
• DEM loading: the nominal 300 MPa plate reaction includes damping; a revised protocol is
planned
• MPM: plastic deformation, with compaction agreement dependent on initialization and loading
protocol
• Voxel transport: ideal-interface calculations with grid dependence and unresolved absolute
conductivity
• Mixing: unresolved diagnostic mixing-index contrast under an unmet overlap condition;
exploratory cohesion results without a verdict
• Final model-validation verdicts remain pending
